Sales Analysis Dashboard
Project Overview
The Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to summarize sales performance and help users explore revenue, order volume, product demand, and sales channels. It presents key metrics and charts in one view, with slicers that allow users to filter the analysis by date, category, city, and product.

Dashboard KPIs
The dashboard currently displays these headline metrics:
- Total Quantity of Products: 142
- Total Revenue: $146,550.00
- Total Orders: 30
These figures reflect the data and filter selections shown in the dashboard screenshot. They may change when filters are applied.

Visualizations
Visualization	What it shows
Revenue by Category	Compares revenue from Electronics, Home, and Stationery.
Revenue by City	Compares revenue across Islamabad, Karachi, and Lahore.
Online vs Store Sales	Shows the proportion of sales attributed to online and in-store channels.
Top 5 Products	Highlights the highest-revenue products.
Monthly Sales	Shows the distribution of sales across months.
Product's Quantity	Displays the quantity sold for a selected product.


Interactive Filters
Use the slicers on the right side of the dashboard to filter the results:
- Date: Select a period or month.
- Category: Filter by product category.
- City: Filter by sales location.
- Product: Focus on an individual product.
The KPI cards and charts are intended to update in response to the available filters.
